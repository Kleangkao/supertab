# SuperTab founder pitch

About 30–40 seconds. Speak slowly. Record one or two lines per take.

Hi, I'm James.

I'm building SuperTab.

It helps people shop with their Solana wallet.

Tell it what you want.
Find real products.
See what your wallet can cover.

I want to make using crypto feel easier.

The demo is live.
We've tested funding, payment, and refund.

Retail payment is our next step.

Thank you for checking out SuperTab.

## Recording
Look at the camera. Use a quiet room and light facing you. Leave a small pause between lines. A plain background is enough. This is separate from the silent product showcase.
