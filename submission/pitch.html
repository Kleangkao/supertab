Solana Mobile · CLOCK IN

AI shopping that uses Solana assets to complete purchases.

SuperTab is an Android app for people who hold Solana and want one coherent path from a product request to a paid merchant order.

Problem

The money is in the wallet. The store is not.

Finding a product, checking a balance, converting assets, approving exact terms, paying a merchant, and proving the order are still separate jobs. SuperTab keeps that whole journey visible.

Who it is for

A person with a Seeker, not a trading desk.

They can say “black polo under $50,” change their mind, and see a real listing before anyone asks them to sign.

What works today

Live shopping and funding review.

  • Real Shopify and eBay results, shortlist, and comparison.
  • Catalog price shown separately from shipping and tax.
  • Solana balance reads and DFlow conversion quotes.
  • Exact-term approval when a merchant total is verified.
  • A labeled local rehearsal for payment and order stages.

Honest limits

Retail payment is prepared, not live.

Card-rail sandbox access and Shopify order-complete permission are still blocked. Opening a merchant link does not pay. A separate owner-controlled devnet order was paid and refunded; it is not a purchase from the selected retailer.

On the Seeker check, the wallet app opened and then could not load the wallet. A connected balance was not read. Emulator checks covered install, launch, and live search.

Next

Unlock the blocked payment and order rails.

With Bridge/Stripe sandbox access and Shopify order capability, the same journey can move from rehearsal into a real merchant payment without changing the product goal.
