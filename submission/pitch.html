Solana Mobile · CLOCK IN

Shop with what the wallet can cover.

SuperTab is an Android app for people who hold Solana and want a normal way to look for something to buy.

Problem

The money is in the wallet. The store is not.

Finding a product, checking a balance, converting assets, and paying a merchant are still separate jobs. SuperTab starts with the first ones a shopper can trust today.

Who it is for

A person with a Seeker, not a trading desk.

They can say “black polo under $50,” change their mind, and see a real listing before anyone asks them to sign.

What the phone does

Search, refine, then look at the gap.

  • Real Shopify and eBay results.
  • Catalog price shown separately from shipping and tax.
  • Mobile Wallet Adapter for a read-only balance check.
  • A public address still works if the wallet is unavailable.

Solana

Read funds. Do not pretend the store was paid.

Retail checkout stays a quote. A separate owner-controlled devnet order was paid and refunded, and the app can open that record. It is not a purchase from another retailer.

On the Seeker check, the wallet app opened and then could not load the wallet. A connected balance was not read.

Roadmap

Next is a card rail for ordinary stores.

The installed app is the shopping and funding review. Paying a merchant, issuing a card, and publishing in the dApp Store come after this proof.
